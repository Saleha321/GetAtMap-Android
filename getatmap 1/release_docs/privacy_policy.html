Privacy Policy for GetAtMap

Effective Date: October 18, 2026
Application Name: GetAtMap
Package Name: com.aistudio.getatmap.vjnr
Website: https://getatmap.com

Core Commitment: GetAtMap is a map-based local marketplace and discovery app. We access your device location strictly in the foreground to show nearby listings. We do not track your location in the background, we do not sell your location data to data brokers, and all communication is encrypted over HTTPS.

1. Information We Collect and Why

We collect the minimal data necessary to provide a smooth, local marketplace experience:

  • Foreground Location (Precise & Approximate GPS): When you open and actively use GetAtMap, the app requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. This is used solely to determine your relative geographic position, display items, vehicles, and services situated closest to you, and calculate driving/walking distances.
  • User-Uploaded Photos: When you create or update an ad/listing, you may select photos using your device's system photo picker. Only images you explicitly select are uploaded to our secure servers.
  • Technical & Connectivity Information: Network status (Wi-Fi or cellular) is checked to maintain reliable connection to the GetAtMap platform and alert you if the device goes offline.

2. How Location Data Is Handled (Google Play Compliance)

  • Foreground Only: Location is accessed strictly while you are actively interacting with the app. No background location tracking occurs when the app is closed or minimized.
  • No Data Brokering: We do NOT sell, rent, license, or monetize your location data or personal info to third-party ad networks or brokers.
  • Secure Web Transmission (HTTPS): All location data passed to the GetAtMap web services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Plain HTTP cleartext traffic is disabled.
  • Domain Restriction: The internal JavaScript location bridge (window.AndroidLocation) is cryptographically restricted and functions strictly on verified getatmap.com domains. External third-party websites opened in the browser cannot access this native bridge.

3. Photo & Media Permissions

We use the standard Android System Photo Picker (zero broad storage permissions) so that you remain in complete control over which photos are shared when posting classifieds.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You can revoke location permission at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > GetAtMap > Permissions > Location > Deny). If you wish to delete your account or any listings, you can request full data removal at any time by contacting us.
